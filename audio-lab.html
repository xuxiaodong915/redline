DRIVE

Hold W / ↑ for full throttle, S / ↓ to brake. Manual RPM mode derives speed & wind from rpm and gear.

GEARBOX AUTO

Upshift = ~50 ms ignition cut, mechanical click, exhaust pop. Downshift = longer cut, clunk and a heavier backfire.

TEST SIGNALS

Use the buttons to audition each layer: quickshifter cuts, metallic crash, stereo near-miss whoosh and the start beep.

Crash is synthesised from filtered noise bursts, metallic ring partials and a sub-bass thud — intensity 0–1.
